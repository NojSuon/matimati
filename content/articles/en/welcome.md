Today we're opening the Mati Mati magazine: a place to read, at your own pace, about the Indigenous languages of Mexico and, little by little, of other regions of the world.

## What you'll find

- **Words**, with their meaning, how they're used, and the language and variety they come from.
- **Stories** about the languages, the communities that speak them and the people who teach them.
- **Ideas** to better understand how they work: writing, sounds, verbs.
- Free **tools** to practice, like our [verb conjugator](https://nojsuon.github.io/coniugatio/en/).

## A word to start with

<p class="word"><span class="lang" lang="nah">Tlasojkamati</span> “thank you” in Nahuatl</p>

You say it to thank someone for a favor, a gift or a shared meal. We write it here in the SEP (Mexico's Ministry of Education) spelling; it may vary by region.
